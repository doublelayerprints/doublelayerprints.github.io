She walks in greeting the whole room at the top of her voice: a princess with nothing shy about her. Yellow and orange gown, crown on her head, and a dress that alone carries a good chunk of the print, between the play of colors and every tiny skirt detail.

Then she pairs up with Luigi: yellow next to green works even better than we expected. And she doesn't seem to mind having a green plumber all to herself.
