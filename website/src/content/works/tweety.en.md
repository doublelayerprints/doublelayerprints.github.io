Television's most protected canary: bright yellow, a black tuft, blue eyes and the face of someone who never doubted who would win. Sitting the way he is, he looks like the only character on the shelf actually taking a break.

Bright yellow forgives nothing, and that's exactly why this one makes us proud: a round, smooth shape, the black tuft planted right on top of the head and a soft gaze toward whoever is watching. And no cat has touched him so far.
