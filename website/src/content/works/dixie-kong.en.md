The little ape with the blonde corkscrew ponytail permanently up: magenta beret and top, and the air of someone who dances even while standing still. That tail was the part we feared most — it has to come out round, continuous and pointed — instead it came out smooth from root to tip.

She holds the scene on her own, but once Diddy Kong steps in, it turns into a duet. Him in red, her in magenta: the contrast does half the work, and on the shelf they light each other up.
